# Attuned 1.4.0 — "The Tideborn Update" Design

Target: Minecraft 26.1.2, Fabric Loader 0.19.2+, mod version `1.4.0`.
Baseline: 1.3.0 (Focus Reliquary, builds, 61 Foci, 4 affinities + Discord,
4 Pacts, 7 Apex capstones, Attunement Altar, Altar of Reweaving).

## Theme

1.3.0 made loadouts effortless. 1.4.0 gives players reasons to keep building:
a new ocean-depth faction, riskier budget math, longer-term Pact goals, and
quality-of-life that makes the existing systems easier to live with.

Headline order (also the implementation order):

1. **The Tideborn** — fifth faction, 6 new Foci, Undertow Pact.
2. **Ashen Debt expansion: Cursed Foci** — 3 negative-cost Foci with drawbacks.
   (Foci total after both: 61 + 6 + 3 = **70**.)
3. **Pact Trials** — advancement chains that unlock a 4th Pact tier.
4. **Reweaving II: Affinity Looms** — reroll a Focus within its affinity.
5. **Build sharing + build hotkeys** — Reliquary exports and quick-apply keys.
6. **HUD freedom** — position/scale/anchor client config.
7. **PvP stance inspect** — read another player's gem at a glance.

Each feature below is independently shippable; cut from the bottom of this
list first if 1.4.0 needs to shrink.

---

## 1. The Tideborn (faction + Foci + Pact)

### Goal

Add `attuned:tideborn` as the faction of those who stopped coming back up.
Seafarers carry memory shore-to-shore; Offshore salvage what sank; the
Tideborn live where the light ends. The faction proves out deep-ocean
traversal, pressure, and guardian/drowned counterplay as a content lane.

### Explicitly NOT a fifth affinity

The four-affinity counter cycle (`Affinity` enum, its codec, `beats()`,
HUD gem sprites, Apex capstone mapping, Pact mapping) is load-bearing and
pinned by many contract tests. A fifth affinity would force a 5-cycle
rebalance and break the rock-paper-scissors readability. The Tideborn are a
**faction** like Offshore/Seafarers/Unseen: mostly neutral Foci, with two
affinity-tagged exceptions to feed existing Pacts. The "outside the cycle"
fantasy is already owned by Discord; Tideborn does not duplicate it.

### New Foci (6)

All `"faction": "attuned:tideborn"`. Names/keys follow the existing
`<name>_focus` convention; every Focus gets item JSON, model, texture,
lang entries (name + lore 1 + lore 2 + effect), focus definition JSON,
journal/reference text, and loot-table inclusion.

| Focus | Affinity | Cost | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lungs_of_brine_focus` | neutral | 2 | Passive: Water Breathing while below sea level (y < 63); brief Haste after surfacing from 30+ s submerged. |
| `pressure_hide_focus` | Bastion | 3 | Passive: +2 armor and knockback resistance while underwater or in rain; nothing on dry land under clear sky. |
| `lure_light_focus` | neutral | 2 | Passive: hostile aquatic mobs (drowned, guardians) target you last while sneaking underwater; small Luck while fishing at night. |
| `riptide_heart_focus` | Zephyr | 3 | Passive: Dolphin's Grace while sprint-swimming; hostile hits while swimming grant 2 s Speed on exit. |
| `abyssal_echo_focus` | neutral | 3 | **Active** (single-active-ability system): 6 s pulse that outlines nearby sea-floor containers/spawners through walls within 16 blocks (reuse `TremorOreOutlines` rendering path with a new tag), 45 s cooldown. |
| `tidebinder_focus` | neutral | 4 | **Active**: 8 s "Undertow Veil" — hostile melee attackers are briefly slowed (riptide drag), you swim at full speed; 90 s cooldown. Counterplay anchor of the faction. |

Behavior IDs: `attuned:lungs_of_brine`, `attuned:pressure_hide`,
`attuned:lure_light`, `attuned:riptide_heart`, `attuned:abyssal_echo`,
`attuned:tidebinder`. Active foci implement `hasActiveAbility()` /
`abilityCooldownTicks()` / `onAbility` exactly like `HarpoonBehavior`.

### Undertow Pact (5th Pact)

`Pact` is an enum keyed by affinity (`Optional<Affinity>`); Untethered
already proves the affinity-empty case. Add:

- `UNDERTOW("undertow", Optional.empty(), ChatFormatting.DARK_AQUA)`
- **Wake condition** (new, faction-based — requires extending the pact
  resolution to count active Foci by faction, a small addition to
  `Pacts.activeAffinityCounts`-adjacent logic): 3+ active Tideborn Foci.
- Tier bonuses (mirroring existing Pact tier shapes):
  - T1 (3 Foci): permanent conduit-power-like vision underwater (no mining speed).
  - T2 (4 Foci): exhaustion from swimming reduced; depth strider behavior.
  - T3 (5 Foci): once per 60 s, lethal damage taken while underwater leaves you at 1 HP and grants 3 s Resistance ("the tide refuses you").
- Death message entries in `PactDeathMessages` for flavor parity.

Wake-condition precedence: affinity Pacts resolve first; Undertow only
wakes when no affinity Pact is active (same slot, no stacking), matching
how Untethered yields today.

### Capstone interaction

No new capstone in this pass. Undertow + Apex resolves to the existing
neutral path (Stillpoint) — the Tideborn build is a neutral build.
A `LEVIATHAN` capstone is a 1.5.0 candidate once the faction proves fun.

### Lore & journal

- Lang: `faction.attuned.tideborn`: `The Tideborn`.
- Journal: one new chapter "Tideborn" (3 pages: who they are, the six Foci,
  the Undertow Pact). Chapter indices are now guarded by the static
  fail-fast check in `AttunementJournalScreen` — append pages at the end of
  `PAGES` and the new chapter entry with the correct first index.
- Suggested tone lines:
  - `The drowned forgot how to want the surface. The Tideborn never knew.`
  - `Pressure is just the deep deciding whether to keep you.`

### Loot

Every new Focus joins the existing survival loot pools (vanilla structure
injects, fishing treasure, archaeology, trial rewards) via the same
loot-table plumbing checked by `AttunedLootCompatibilityTest`. Tideborn
Foci weight slightly heavier in ocean-adjacent tables (shipwreck, buried
treasure, ocean ruin, monument-adjacent) if per-table weighting already
exists; otherwise uniform.

### Tests

- `TidebornContentContractTest`: faction translation, 6 focus JSONs with
  `attuned:tideborn`, behavior ids registered, costs match this spec.
- `UndertowPactContractTest`: enum entry, wake at 3 Tideborn Foci, yields
  to affinity Pacts, T3 cheat-death cooldown state cleaned on disconnect
  (extend `RuntimeCleanupContractTest`).
- Behavior unit tests per active Focus (cooldown, fail-without-cooldown
  conditions) following `HarpoonBehaviorContractTest` shape.
- `FocusDataConsistencyTest` will automatically sweep lang/model/texture
  for the new items — run early, it catches most omissions.
- README focus count 61 → 67 (the `verify_repository.py` gate enforces it).

## 2. Cursed Foci (Ashen Debt expansion)

### Goal

Foci that *refund* budget but carry a real drawback: "deal with the
devil" builds. Three Foci in the existing `attuned:ashen_forge` /
`attuned:revenant` lanes.

### Mechanics: negative cost

`FocusDefinition` currently clamps cost to `0..64` (`MIN_COST`/`MAX_COST`,
codec `intRange`). Change:

- `MIN_COST = -8`. Codec range becomes `-8..64`.
- `BudgetResolver` already sums costs; negative values subtract naturally.
  **Add one invariant**: the *running* total used for dormancy resolution
  is clamped at ≥ 0 — a player cannot bank refunds beyond their capacity
  floor (no "infinite budget from stacking curses").
- Unique flag is mandatory (`"unique": true`) for all cursed Foci so the
  refund cannot be farmed by duplicates.
- Altar readout, `AttunementReadout`, Foci HUD, and tooltips must render
  negative costs as `+N` *refund* (e.g. `Refunds 2 attunement`) instead of
  a misleading `-2 cost`. Tooltip code path: `AttunedTooltips`.

### The three Cursed Foci

| Focus | Cost | Faction | Drawback / payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| `splintered_crown_focus` | -2 | ashen_forge | +refund; you take +15% fire damage and lit furnaces/campfires within 8 blocks aggro nearby hostiles to you first. |
| `hollow_chime_focus` | -3 | revenant | +refund; 10% of healing received is forfeited; on death you drop an extra "echo" XP penalty (10% more XP lost). |
| `leaden_oath_focus` | -2 | ashen_forge | +refund; permanent Slowness I while above 80% health — you must stay hurt to move freely. |

Drawbacks are passive behaviors (`FocusBehavior` hooks already cover
damage events via the combat path used by Mossheart/Kilnward-style Foci).

### UX guardrails

- Cursed Foci render with a distinct dark-red cost chip in the Focus panel
  and Reliquary (one new sprite, reuse `AffinityColors` plumbing).
- Journal "Curses" page (1 page) in the existing Lore chapter explaining
  the floor-clamp rule.

### Tests

- Codec round-trip with negative cost (`FocusHolderCodecRoundTripTest`
  pattern).
- `BudgetResolverTest` additions: refund math, ≥0 clamp, dormancy order
  with mixed negative/positive costs.
- Contract tests: all cursed Foci are `unique`, drawback behaviors
  registered, tooltip renders refund wording.
- Reweaving exclusion: cursed Foci are NOT valid Reweaving outputs
  (`ReweavingResultPickerTest` addition) — curses are found, not made.

### Risk

Highest-risk feature of the release (touches budget core). Implement
behind thorough `BudgetResolverTest` coverage first (TDD), and keep the
clamp logic in one place.

## 3. Pact Trials (advancement chains + Tier 4)

### Goal

Give awakened Pacts a long-term goal: a per-Pact challenge chain that
permanently unlocks a small **Tier 4** bonus for that Pact.

### Design

Existing precedent: `pact_pyresworn_challenge.json` advancement already
exists — generalize the pattern to all five Pacts.

Trials (tracked server-side; progress persists via a new
`PactTrialProgress` attachment, mirroring `AttunedAttachments` patterns):

| Pact | Trial | Tier 4 reward (small, passive) |
|---|---|---|
| Pyresworn | Ignite 50 hostile mobs while the Pact is awake | Fire ticks on your melee hits last +1 s |
| Stoneheart | Block/absorb 500 damage while awake | +1 armor toughness while awake |
| Windrunner | Travel 10,000 blocks while awake | +5% speed while awake |
| Untethered | Win 25 combats at Apex resonance while awake | Resonance decays 10% slower |
| Undertow | Survive 30 min cumulative underwater while awake | T3 cheat-death cooldown 60 s → 45 s |

Rules:

- Progress only accrues while the matching Pact is awake.
- Completion fires an advancement (toast + chat line in Pact color) and is
  permanent per player (survives Pact switching).
- Tier 4 applies only while that Pact is awake — switching Pacts switches
  which Tier 4 you benefit from.

### Implementation notes

- Counters: hook the same events the Pacts already listen to (damage,
  movement via periodic position delta on the existing server tick cadence
  — reuse the 5-tick sync cadence pattern from `FocusAbilityState`, do not
  add a new per-tick walk).
- Persistence: data-attachment with codec, cleaned on player forget
  (extend `AttunedPlayerCleanup` registration like the cooldown maps).
- Advancements: `data/attuned/advancement/attunement/pact_<name>_trial.json`
  granted in code on completion (the criteria are custom counters, not
  vanilla triggers).
- HUD: no new HUD. The Attunement Journal Pacts chapter gains a progress
  line per Pact (`journal.attuned.trial.<pact>` lang keys), reading the
  synced attachment. One new clientbound payload `PactTrialStatusPayload`
  (sync on change only, mirroring `FocusAbilityStatusPayload`'s
  last-sent dedupe).

### Tests

- Attachment codec round-trip; cleanup on disconnect/forget.
- Counter unit tests per trial (accrue while awake, frozen while asleep).
- Tier 4 effect contract tests (applies awake, absent otherwise).
- Advancement JSON existence + grant-on-completion contract.

## 4. Reweaving II: Affinity Looms

### Goal

A late-game shard sink: reroll a Focus into a *different random Focus of
the same affinity* (or neutral → neutral) at the Altar of Reweaving, at
escalating cost.

### Design

- New altar mode: place a Focus + N Attunement Shards. Output: a random
  different Focus with the same `Optional<Affinity>` value, excluding:
  the input itself, cursed Foci, and anything the existing
  `ReweavingResultPicker` already excludes.
- Escalating cost: 1st reroll of a given session costs 1 shard, then 2,
  then 3 (cap 3). Counter resets on altar close — per-screen-session,
  no persistence (keeps state simple and predictable).
- UI: extend `ReweavingScreen` (135 lines, room to grow) with a second
  input well for shards and a cost readout. Server validates everything;
  the client only renders.
- Neutral pool is large (30+ Foci), affinity pools are ~6–10 each — both
  sizes give satisfying variance.

### Tests

- `ReweavingResultPickerTest` additions: same-affinity guarantee, input
  exclusion, cursed exclusion, escalation sequence 1→2→3→3, reset on
  close.
- Screen contract test for the new well/readout strings.
- Server-side validation test: rejects mismatched shards, rejects empty
  input, no item dupe on disconnect mid-reweave (extend
  `RuntimeCleanupContractTest`).

## 5. Build sharing + build hotkeys

### 5a. Build sharing

- **Export**: in the Reliquary screen, a small `Share` button beside a
  selected build copies a build code to the clipboard:
  `attuned:v1:<base64 of name + ordered item id list>` (the existing
  `FocusPreset(String name, List<String> slots)` record serializes
  directly; cap decoded name at the existing 32 chars, slots at 6).
- **Import**: an `Import` button reads the clipboard, validates the
  prefix/version, shows the build name in the field, and saves it as a
  normal preset (subject to the existing `MAX_PRESETS = 9` cap and the
  server-side normalization in `AttunedAttachments`).
- Validation is server-authoritative: the client sends the decoded
  preset through the existing `SavePresetPayload` path — no new
  trust surface. Unknown item ids are kept as-is (presets already
  tolerate missing Foci with the "could not find" report on Apply).
- No written-book variant in this pass (clipboard is one keypress and
  works across servers; books add NBT/component surface for little gain).

### 5b. Build hotkeys

- Three new keybinds, unbound by default (matching the HUD toggles):
  `key.attuned.apply_build_1/2/3` → sends the existing
  `ApplyPresetPayload(index)` for indices 0–2.
- Server side already validates indices and inventory sourcing — zero new
  server code.
- Overlay message on apply reuses the preset-apply feedback path.

### Tests

- Round-trip test: preset → code → preset equality (name, slots, order).
- Malformed-code rejection (bad prefix, bad base64, >6 slots, >32 name).
- Keybind contract test (lang keys, unbound default, payload index range)
  mirroring `FociHudContractTest`'s keybind sections.

## 6. HUD freedom (position / scale / anchor)

### Goal

Stop fighting minimap mods. Both HUDs (Combat + Foci) get user-positionable
anchors via `attuned-client.json` — config only, no drag-and-drop editor in
this pass.

### Design

Extend `AttunedClientConfig` (record + JSON, same `booleanOr`-style
tolerant parsing) with:

```json
{
  "hud_anchor": "bottom_right",     // bottom_right | bottom_left | top_right | top_left
  "hud_offset_x": 0,                // px, applied after anchor
  "hud_offset_y": 0,
  "hud_scale": 1.0                  // 0.5..2.0, snapped to 0.25 steps
}
```

- One anchor/offset/scale set applies to the Combat+Foci HUD cluster as a
  unit (they're designed as a sidecar pair; independent positioning breaks
  the secondary-sidecar collision logic in `CombatHud`).
- `sidecarX/Y` and `FociHud.primarySidecarX/Y` route through a new shared
  `HudAnchor.resolve(screenW, screenH, w, h, config)` helper so the
  collision/fallback logic stays in one place. The default config must
  reproduce today's exact positions (golden values asserted in tests).
- Scale wraps the draw in the GUI pose stack scale; tooltips/click areas
  are unaffected (HUDs are render-only).
- Out of scope: in-game positioning screen (1.5.0 candidate; config file
  covers the modpack use case now).

### Tests

- Config round-trip with new keys; defaults preserve current behavior
  (assert resolved x/y for a 1920×1080 GUI equals today's constants).
- Anchor math unit tests for all four corners + clamping at small GUI
  sizes.
- Existing pinned strings in `FociHudContractTest`
  (`screenW - hudWidth - SCREEN_MARGIN`, `primarySidecarX`) must be
  preserved or the contract tests updated in the same commit — check
  before refactoring.

## 7. PvP stance inspect

### Goal

Make the affinity rock-paper-scissors readable against *players*, not just
mobs.

### Design

- The Combat HUD's target slot already renders mob affinities via
  `MobAffinities`. Extend `targetedStance` to handle `Player` targets:
  committed affinity, Discord, capstone, and apex-armed state — the same
  `OwnStance`-shape data, but for the crosshair-picked player.
- Server-authoritative: a player's stance is derived from their synced
  attachment data. If the target player's Foci attachment isn't synced to
  observers today, add a minimal `StanceSummaryPayload` broadcast
  (affinity ordinal + discord flag + capstone ordinal + apex bool ≈ 4
  bytes) on stance change, tracked-player scoped — do NOT sync full
  inventories to other clients.
- Privacy/config: server-side toggle `pvp_stance_inspect` in
  `AttunedConfig` (default on); when off, player targets render the
  neutral gem.
- Range and rendering reuse the existing 12-block crosshair rules.

### Tests

- Payload codec round-trip; broadcast on change only (dedupe like
  `FocusAbilityState.sync`).
- Stance summary matches the player's actual resolution (server unit
  test against `Attunement.resolution`).
- Config-off renders neutral (contract test on the gating branch).

- 9 new item textures + models (6 Tideborn, 3 Cursed) via the existing
  `generate_ui_art.py` / asset_customizer pipeline.

- ~40 lang entries.

### Test surface (rough)

~15 new/extended test classes; every feature lands with its contract tests
in the same commit (repo convention).

### Suggested milestones

| Phase | Contents | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| A | HUD freedom (6) + build hotkeys (5b) | low — pure client/config |
| B | Build sharing (5a) + PvP inspect (7) | low-med — small payloads |
| C | Tideborn faction, Foci, Undertow Pact (1) | med — content volume |
| D | Pact Trials (3) + Reweaving II (4) | med — new attachment + UI |
| E | Cursed Foci (2) | high — budget core; last so it can slip to 1.4.1 |

Phases A+B alone are a respectable 1.3.x patch if 1.4.0 needs to ship
early; C is the version-defining content.

### Out of scope for 1.4.0

- Fifth affinity / cycle rebalance.
- Leviathan capstone for Undertow.
- In-game HUD positioning editor.
- Written-book build sharing.
- Per-HUD independent anchors.
- Offshore Pact (Offshore stays a two-Focus faction until it earns more).
